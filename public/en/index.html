Questions about advertising with creators.

What is NOIZER, and how does the advertising work?

NOIZER connects brands and advertising agencies with content creators, placing branded banners in Reels and YouTube Shorts. We manage the campaign from creator selection through to completion, including performance data collection and reporting.

What are creator video banners best suited for?

The format is suited to reach and brand awareness campaigns, including upper funnel budgets. Your brand appears within the creator’s content. Sales or conversions are not guaranteed.

How is campaign pricing calculated?

Payment is based on views, within a budget agreed in advance. NOIZER collects performance data and reports to the advertiser as part of campaign management.

How is a banner different from a dedicated sponsored video?

With NOIZER, the banner appears in a video the creator already plans to post. Creators keep their own style, while your brand gets a place in the frame. We provide managed campaigns, rather than a self-service advertising platform.

How can creators join?

Get in touch through our creator contact section with a profile link and a little about your content. NOIZER reviews the fit for campaigns and brands; there is no automatic platform signup.
